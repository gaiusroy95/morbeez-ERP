The Iron Rule

Money, invoices, ledgers, inventory quantities, credit decisions, accounting entries and financial adjustments remain under deterministic ERP control.

PART 1 — BUSINESS MODEL

Morbeez ERP is designed for businesses that:

- Buy perishable products
- Procure from farmers, suppliers, agents or markets
- Process or aggregate products
- Store products temporarily
- Move products through vehicles/cold chain
- Sell to businesses/customers
- Deliver multiple orders per trip
- Handle KG-based transactions
- Experience shrinkage and quality loss
- Operate with customer credit
- Pay suppliers on different terms
- Need strong cash-flow control
- Need real-time profitability
- Need customer collection management
- Need AI-driven operational communication

Primary example:

«Chicken / Meat Distribution»

Architecture remains flexible enough to support:

- Poultry
- Chicken
- Meat
- Fish
- Seafood
- Eggs
- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Other perishable goods

The product engine must therefore support configurable perishability characteristics rather than hard-code one commodity.
